Before you book a first lesson預約第一堂課之前

1Verify who they are.確認對方身分。 Ask for ID and check it matches the profile.請對方出示證件並核對是否與檔案相符。
2Ask for a police record certificate.索取良民證。 Request it from the tutor directly. We never see it.直接向家教索取，我們不會取得。
3Start in public or online.先在公共場所或線上開始。 A parent stays present for early lessons.前幾堂課請家長在場。
4Check the tutor may legally tutor.確認對方可合法家教。 Some work permits don't allow private lessons.部分工作許可不允許私人授課。

Questions常見問題

What if I find a tutor in two weeks?如果兩週就找到家教怎麼辦？

Your membership still covers re-hires, new subjects and siblings for the rest of the 12 months.會員資格在剩餘的 12 個月內仍可用於更換家教、新增科目與兄弟姊妹。

Do you take a commission on lessons?課程費用會被抽成嗎？

No. You pay the tutor directly. Tutors never pay us anything.不會。您直接付費給家教，家教也不需付任何費用給我們。

Does it renew automatically?會自動續約嗎？

Never. We'll remind you before it ends, and renewal is always your choice.不會。到期前我們會提醒您，是否續約由您決定。

Can I look around before paying?付費之前可以先看看嗎？

No. Tutors agreed to share their profiles only with paying members. We show sample profiles on our site so you know what to expect.不行。家教僅同意將檔案提供給付費會員查看。我們在網站上提供範例檔案，讓您了解內容。

Do you check the tutors?你們會審查家教嗎？

No. We do not run background checks on tutors, families or schools. We're a connection website, so please use the checklist and verify everything directly.不會。我們不對家教、家長或學校進行背景調查。我們僅為連結平台，請使用檢查清單並親自確認一切。
