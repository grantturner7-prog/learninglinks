How we're different我們的不同

Free for educators, always教師永遠免費

Create a profile, get found, and never pay us anything.建立個人檔案、被看見，完全不需要付費給我們。

No placement fees沒有仲介費

Schools and families pay one flat membership for 12 months. We never take a cut of a salary or a lesson.學校與家長只需支付一次 12 個月的固定會員費。我們不抽取薪資或課程費用。

You control what's shown公開內容由您決定

Only paying members can see your profile. You choose what to share, and can hide or delete it in one click.只有付費會員能看到您的檔案。分享什麼由您決定，並可一鍵隱藏或刪除。

No sensitive documents不保存敏感文件

We never store IDs, criminal records or financial data. Share those directly, only when you choose.我們不儲存身分證件、良民證或財務資料，由您自行決定是否、何時直接提供。
