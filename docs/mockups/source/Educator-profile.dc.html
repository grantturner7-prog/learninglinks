Before you hire or book聘僱或預約之前

Please read:請注意： We only connect people. We do not run background checks on anyone. Please complete all checks and follow all government policies and procedures yourself.我們僅提供連結服務，不對任何人進行背景調查。請自行完成所有查核，並遵守所有政府政策與程序。
  • Confirm identity and right to work directly.親自確認身分與工作許可。
  • Ask for a police record certificate yourself. We never see it.自行向教師索取良民證，我們不會取得。
  • Check references and try a short trial class.確認推薦人，並安排短暫試教。
  • For children, meet in a public place or online with a parent present.涉及兒童時，請在公共場所或由家長在場的線上進行。
