# Competitor check: Tealit (tealit.com), 17 Nov 2026

**Question:** what licences does Tealit hold, and do we need the same?

**Short answer:** I could not find any licence. No permit number appears on the site or in its advertiser contract. I could not query Taiwan's company registry or the Ministry of Labor's licensed-agency list from this environment, so the legal status is **unverified**. What Tealit shows is a company that sells **advertising** and calls its service "online recruitment" (網路徵才服務), not "placement".

## What this does and does not tell us

- **Not shown:** any 私立就業服務機構許可證 (private employment service institution permit), the operator's tax ID, or its registered business items.
- **Likely approach (my inference, unverified):** run through an ordinary Taiwanese limited company, describe the service as advertising and information, forbid agencies, and disclaim any guarantee. That is close to your model, and to the disclaimer wording we use.
- **Not proof of legality.** A competitor operating for 20+ years does not show that it is licensed or exempt. It only shows the model exists.
- **Pricing benchmark:** Tealit charges employers up to NT$25,000 a year for an ad plus VIP access to profiles. Your NT$10,000 a year is well below that.

## What Taiwan requires (from search summaries; confirm with a lawyer)

- Under Article 34 of the Employment Service Act, a **private employment service institution** needs a permit and a licence certificate before it does placement work, and the permit must be renewed. [Employment Service Act](https://laws.mol.gov.tw/Eng/FLAWQRY01.aspx?fcode=A008) · [summary](https://www.rootlaw.com.tw/LetterContent.aspx?LCode=23&SOID=221685)
- A Hong Kong firm's guide (2021) says licensed firms need staff with a Taiwan employment-services professional certificate. [guide](https://kaizencpa.com/download/tw/Guide%20to%20Taiwan%20Employment%20Services%20License%20(CHT).pdf)
- **Open question that decides everything:** whether an online directory that charges employers for access to profiles counts as "placement". Tealit's structure suggests it is treated as advertising, but only the competent authority or a lawyer can say so for LearningLinks.

## How to verify (5 minutes each)

1. **Company registry (經濟部商工登記公示資料, findbiz.nat.gov.tw):** search 博客國際整合行銷有限公司 and 卓越科技教育股份有限公司. Check the 統一編號 and the 所營事業 (business items). If either lists an employment-service item, that is a lead.
2. **Ministry of Labor licensed-agency list:** search the same two names in the private employment service institution list (全國就業e網, or the local labour bureau's roster). Some cities publish rosters on data.gov.tw, for example [Yilan](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/42319).
3. **Ask the authority in writing.** As a Taichung company, ask the Taichung City Government Labor Bureau (臺中市政府勞工局) for a written answer on whether LearningLinks' model (a paid directory, flat fee, no placement fee, no matching by us) needs a 私立就業服務機構 permit. A written reply from the authority is the best protection. [verify the right office with your lawyer]
